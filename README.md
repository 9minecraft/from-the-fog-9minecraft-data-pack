# From The Fog — Herobrine Stalks Your Survival World

From the Fog 9minecraft Data Pack brings the classic Herobrine legend straight into your world. This data pack turns regular survival into a slow, chilling mystery where an unseen watcher stalks you from the shadows. You begin to notice weird signs around your home, like missing torches, cut trees, and small stone pyramids. Over time, a figure with glowing white eyes appears at the edge of your view before vanishing into thick fog.

## What this build is

An unofficial rebuild of **Lunar Eclipse Studios**'s original work, kept running on current Minecraft releases by 9Minecraft Studios. Not affiliated with the original author and not endorsed by them.

Keeping a pack alive across releases is not a copy-paste job: each Minecraft version bumps the pack format and shuffles texture and model paths, so the tree gets re-mapped and every asset re-checked against the version it is built for. Anything the new release dropped is rebuilt rather than left pointing at a path that no longer exists.
